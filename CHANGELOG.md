# Changelog

## v1.0 (final, uniform width)

Collects everything found while building the v0.x prototype:

- **ATX power supply** instead of SFX: cradle in the bottom plate (no rib in front of the modular sockets), full rear
  cut-out with the 4 standard 6-32 holes, honeycomb grille in the right lower wall in front of the PSU fan.
- **Drive fan cable:** larger pass-through at the back of the fan box (16 × 12 mm), fan plug fits through; the cable
  runs flat under the backplane holder.
- **Feet** moved inward so no foot sits on a drive-fan screw head.
- **Board standoffs:** no standoff at hole S, plain support under the extra hole near the front-left corner.
- **Tool access:** thumb-screw tabs and front tabs moved, gap in the display stop rib — all 12 long M3×20 screws are
  now reachable with a straight key.
- **Mid plate:** 3.8 mm clearance holes for the long wall screws (more tolerance).
- **Switch bezel** (option B) for a round Ø 20 mm rocker switch in the finger window.
- Engraving on the backplane holder now reads BOTTOM.

**Do not mix v1.0 with v0.x prints.** Changed against v0.6: `bottom_plate`, `front_lower`, `rear_lower`,
`side_right_lower`, `mid_plate`, `front_upper`, `side_left_upper`, `side_right_upper`, `top_plate` (tab positions, PSU)
and `backplane_holder` (engraving only). All other files are identical to v0.6.

## v0.6

Backplane holder adjustable in slots (connector position on the backplane is not dimensioned), lifted 8 mm off the
floor, printed clamp washers, M3×12 holder screws; braces hang on the holder.

## v0.5

Two braces behind the backplane holder (it flexed when drives were inserted).

## v0.4

Tray screw holes corrected to SFF-8301 / SFF-8201 (measured from the connector end); hole gauges; printability fixes.

## v0.3

Case 20 mm wider for two 120 mm side fans in the PC module.

## v0.2

Everything screwed with M3 heat-set inserts instead of push-fit joints and threaded rods.

## v0.1

First layout: vertical hot-swap bays, fan under the drives, microATX on top.
